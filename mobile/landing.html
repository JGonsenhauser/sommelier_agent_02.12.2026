Guests find a bottle without a sommelier

Guests scan a QR wherever they are on the property. They tell Jarvis the dish, wine style, budget or mood they have in mind. Every recommendation comes from the wines available at that outlet.

From recommendation to service

Jarvis recommends two wines that fit the guest’s preferences and the outlet’s list. Each recommendation includes a tasting note and why it works for the dish or occasion. The guest can make a confident choice and send the selection directly to the service team.
