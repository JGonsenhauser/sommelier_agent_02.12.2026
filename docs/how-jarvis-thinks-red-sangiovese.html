When you type red sangiovese

No price button is pressed. The words alone are the whole request. On the list as it stands today, those words land on two bottles: a Chianti under $100, then a Super Tuscan between $101 and $160.

red sangiovese

1

Read the words

red Color is locked to red. A white or sparkling bottle cannot come through, even if the name sounds Italian.
sangiovese The named grape is Sangiovese. Both bottles have to be that grape. Pinot, Cabernet, and Nebbiolo are out.

Nothing in the sentence sets a price, a body, a place, or a dish. So the default price rule is allowed to run later.

↓
2

Walk the whole list

272 bottles are in stock. Each one has to pass three gates. Today, 33 survive.

  • In stock, and the inventory count is not zero.
  • The color of the bottle is red.
  • The bottle is Sangiovese: Chianti, Brunello, Vino Nobile, Morellino, Flaccianello, Tignanello, or Solaia. Sassicaia, Ornellaia, Masseto, and Guidalberto are refused even if the cellar file calls them Tuscan blends.
↓
3

Score the 33 that remain

A Chianti is lifted the most. The other Sangiovese lanes get a smaller lift. A Pinot that somehow said Sangiovese is pushed out of the running.

  • Chianti Classico and the other Chiantis: +2.6
  • Vino Nobile: +1.8
  • Tignanello, Solaia, Flaccianello: +1.4
  • Brunello: +1.0, unless the guest also asked for a full body or the cellar
  • The word “sangiovese” sitting on the producer, label, grape, or place adds a smaller match on top

The highest scores are a row of Chianti Classico, all at 3.95. Fontodi 2020 is the first of that row.

↓
4

A style pairing is considered, then set aside

If a price had been chosen, Jarvis would pair two different Sangiovese lanes and two different producers. The top Chianti would sit next to the best bottle that is not a Chianti.

Would have been the pair 2020 Fontodi Chianti Classico, $40, with 2019 Avignonesi Vino Nobile, $35. Same grape, different lane. This pair is not what you see.
Why it is dropped No price was selected. The price rule replaces that pair. Grok is also not allowed to swap the bottles when no price was chosen.

6

Notes are written after the bottles are chosen

The pairing is finished before any tasting note is written. Each note has to name that vintage, that producer, and that place. A 2019 and a 2020 of the same label are not allowed to wear the same note. The email then sends the notes that are on the screen.

There is no dish in “red sangiovese,” so Jarvis does not attach a plate from the menu.

When you type full body cab

No price button. “Full body” locks the weight. “Cab” now locks the grape: cab, cabernet, and cabernet sauvignon are the same word, and only Cabernet labels remain.

full body cab

1

Read the words

full body Body is locked to full. Light reds are refused. Lean whites are refused. Moscato is refused.
cab Cab, cabernet, and cabernet sauvignon are one grape. Both bottles have to be Cabernet. A Barolo, a Syrah, or a Shiraz cannot come through.

Color locks to red with the grape. No price word, so the default price rule can still run at the end. Shiraz is not treated as Syrah. Chard, chardonnay, and the misspelling chardonay are one grape.

↓
2

Walk the whole list

272 bottles are in stock. Cabernet plus full body leaves the Cabernets only. Barolo and the other full reds are out, because the grape is locked.

  • In stock, and the inventory count is not zero.
  • The bottle is Cabernet. The word cabernet has to be on the producer, the label, or the grape.
  • Full body still removes a light red and a lean white, and Moscato.
↓
3

Score what remains

A full red gets +2. Cabernet, Barolo, Brunello, Syrah, and Malbec get another +0.8. The letters “cab” add a smaller match wherever “cabernet” is written on the bottle.

Many bottles tie at 3.13. The list order breaks the tie. Ridge Vineyards Geyserville is first. On this list its grape is recorded as Cabernet Sauvignon, so it is treated as a Cabernet, at $50.

  • A $100 bottle, such as Silver Oak Alexander Valley, sits in neither price band. Under $100 means below 100. The next band starts above $101.
  • Caymus at $90 and Montelena at $70 tie with Geyserville. Geyserville is simply earlier in the ranked list.
